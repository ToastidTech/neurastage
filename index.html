How it works

01

Ingest

Paste or import raw HTML, text, CSV, or markdown. Messy exports welcome.

02

Sanitize

Scripts, tags, boilerplate, and duplicate lines are stripped. Emails, phone numbers, SSNs, and card numbers are redacted.

03

Stage

Clean text is split into overlapping vector-ready chunks with a deterministic JSON payload — copy it or download it.
